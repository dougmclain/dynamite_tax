How it works

Four steps, once a year

Most associations spend a few minutes with us. We handle the forms, the math and the deadlines.

  1. 1

    Send your year-end financials

    Upload your income statement or P&L. We pull out dues, interest, rental income and expenses, then review every number.

FAQ

Common questions

Does our association have to file a tax return?

Most homeowners and condominium associations must file a federal income tax return every year, even when they owe no tax. Many qualify for the simpler Form 1120-H, and we'll confirm whether yours does.

What is Form 1120-H?

A simplified return that qualifying associations can choose each year. Member dues and assessments are not taxed. Other income, such as bank interest or rent from non-members, is taxed at a flat 30% after a $100 specific deduction (32% for timeshare associations).

How do we know if we qualify?

In general, at least 60% of gross income must come from member dues, fees and assessments; at least 90% of spending must go to acquiring, building, managing and maintaining association property; and for condominium and residential associations at least 85% of units must be used as residences. We check all three for you.

When is the return due?

By the 15th day of the fourth month after your association's fiscal year ends: April 15 for calendar-year associations. Form 7004 gives an automatic six-month extension to file, but any tax owed is still due by the original date.

What do you need from us?

Your year-end income statement (P&L), your EIN and mailing address, and last year's return if we didn't prepare it. We'll follow up if anything is unclear.

What does it cost?

A flat fee per association, confirmed in your engagement letter before we start. Any state return is quoted separately. Management companies with several associations can ask us about portfolio pricing.

Ready to take this year's return off your plate?

Tell us your association's name and fiscal year end. We'll reply with an engagement letter that shows your flat fee.
